02 / Teleoperation ESP32 · Arduino Uno

Hand-Gesture-Controlled Vehicle

A wearable teleoperation system combining a sensor-equipped transmitter glove with a 4-wheel robotic chassis. Reads 3-axis pitch and roll angles from an IMU to translate hand gestures into proportional forward, reverse, and turning maneuvers in real time.
